the finding

Travel is set by where the table starts, not how wide it is

Narrowing the table to 62% only took the gap from 193 to 111, because the label still begins at the far left of whatever column it is in. Right-shifting the whole table took it to 61 without narrowing anything.

That is why C and G beat A, B, D and E on your own criterion while also being the only two that keep the value column landing on target. The instinct to narrow was right about the symptom and the fix turned out to be a different move.

Measured, worst case

Reference 193 · A 111 · B 123 · C 61 · G 89 · D 127 · E 127 · F 92.

Worst case, not average: the widest gap in the block, which is always the row with the shortest label. SCHEDULE against a right-aligned tue is the one doing the damage in most of these.

C and G are the same geometry

Both shift the table right and hand the freed half to something else. The only question between them is what deserves that half: a second way of saying tuesday, or the kit.

C's dots are the better picture and duplicate a row that is already there. G's kit is the better use and pushes equipment ahead of the schedule in reading order. If the schedule row came out of C's table, the duplication argument disappears and C wins outright.

D is also the shortest header here

Promoting the estimate to a stat removes the caption line above the table, so D lands the first exercise at 147 pt against everything else's 171. It is the only option that buys height as well as answering the question.

Its travel is poor, though, at 127. If the estimate's weight appeals, the honest move is to take that idea into C's or G's geometry rather than D's.

⚠ check before building E

Four pips can read as judgment

A missing pip in "last four" states a fact, but the app has a standing anti-shame law: regressions render neutral, diffs sum positive movement only, no out-of-band warnings.

A gap in a row of purple squares is exactly the kind of thing that law exists to catch. It is not automatically disqualifying, but it needs a deliberate ruling rather than arriving as a side effect of filling a column.

Unchanged underneath

Names wrap, the node centres on the first line, the ledger prints target beside prev with no delta, the columns pin, Start plays the ignition. The whole 1-family still pins nothing itself, so these header rows scroll away with the title.
