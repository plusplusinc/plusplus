Why the kit tags look like that

The surface law is shape carries what taps: a card data tag is soft r6 with no stroke and is inert, while anything interactive wears the control shape. The kit tags take the soft ground with a ring added. They keep the data tag's fill, so they still read as names rather than buttons, and gain the stroke that says they open something. The muscle groups keep the bare soft fill and stay inert, which is what makes the stroke mean anything.
