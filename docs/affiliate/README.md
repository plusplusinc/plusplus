## The three findings

**Affiliate links do not reach $1M/year as a link on a detail page.** That form
needs ~22 million monthly actives. It becomes arguable at ~1,000,000 and
plausible at ~380,000, but only if the recommendation lands on a four-figure
basket, attribution runs on a discount code rather than a cookie, and
commission comes from a direct deal.

**Commission rate is the least important lever** — the one every affiliate
roundup ranks by, and the smallest multiplier in the model. Order value (10x)
and attribution (3x) both dominate it (2.5x).

**The kit builder is the interesting product.** *"These four pieces open 112
exercises, cheapest unlock first"* is buildable today from `CatalogReach` and
nobody else can build it, because nobody else models equipment as a first-class
thing with reach math over it. It is worth shipping on its own merits as a Pro
feature — and it is the only version of this business that reaches seven
figures.

⚠️ Every commission figure in PROGRAMS.md is **secondary-sourced**: the
merchants' own affiliate pages return HTTP 403 to automated fetches. Confirm at
application time.
